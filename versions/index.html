Strike Zone 1.0latest

2026-10-18

First public release: a complete single-file three.js wave-survival FPS.

  • Single-file three.js arena shooter with three.js pulled from CDN fallbacks (jsDelivr, unpkg, esm.sh).
  • Three weapons: assault rifle, shotgun, pistol, each with its own spread, recoil and reload timing.
  • Wave survival with grunts, scouts and brutes; enemy HP and damage scale with the wave, accuracy never does.
  • Aim down sights (hold right mouse): tighter spread, slower movement, lower sensitivity, scope overlay with magnification readout.
  • Lean left/right with Q and E: the camera slides and rolls around cover while your collision body stays put, so you can shoot around corners without stepping out.
  • Melee with V or F: fixed 62 damage, and a one-shot backstab when a hostile is not facing you.
  • Reload animation (magazine drop, pump cycle, slide rack), staged reload sounds and a reload progress bar.
  • Bullet tracers for both sides: white/yellow for you, red-orange for hostiles, so you can read incoming fire.
  • Destructible map: crates, fuel barrels and low walls take damage from bullets, melee and grenades. Barrels detonate, destroyed cover drops its collider and everything rebuilds on redeploy.
  • Brightened arena with fill and bounce lighting, warmer textures and ACES tone mapping.
  • Out-of-combat health regeneration up to 70%, wave-clear rewards, pickups and a forgiving enemy accuracy cap.
  • HUD: health and armor, ammo, wave, radar, kill feed, hitmarkers, damage direction and a run summary with accuracy, backstabs and props destroyed.
  • Built-in headless test suite: Node mock DOM/three.js harness covering lean, ADS, tracers, reload, melee, destruction and bot balance runs.
