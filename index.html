Headshots deal 2.2× damage. Health regenerates up to 70% after 7s without damage.
Q/E leans your camera and shots around cover — you also peek into enemy line of fire.
Hold RMB to aim down sights: much tighter spread, slower movement, lower mouse sensitivity.

Later waves hit harder and take more bullets, but never aim better.
